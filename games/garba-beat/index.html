How to play

Four moves, one circle

Notes travel around the circle to the beat of the song. Hit them as they reach the ring, keep your combo alive and the whole crowd joins in.

Tap on the beat

Hit each note as it lands on the ring. Time it right for a Perfect.

Hold for a ghoomar spin

Long notes sweep around the circle. Hold all the way through to spin.

Swipe up to jump

Flick up on jump notes. Bigger streaks unlock bigger moves.

Fill the Utsav meter

Build the meter to set off a dhol solo, where every hit scores double.

Dandiya

Strike sticks with your jodi

Dandiya songs pair you up with a jodi. Low strikes, backhands and spins come at you around the circle.

  • Land the same strike as your jodi for a Jodi bonus.
  • Hit the big beats together for Sangat.
  • Watch the circle: sometimes it turns anticlockwise.

Multiplayer

Dance live with friends

Open a room and invite up to five friends. Everyone dances the same song at the same time, with emotes, and Sangat bonuses when the whole group hits the beat.

Pick who you'd like as your jodi in the lobby, or get paired up automatically.
